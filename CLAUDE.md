# Agent entry point

Before doing anything in this repo, read in this order, fully:

1. `docs/HANDOFF.md` — what this is, the owner's rules, current state, next steps.
2. `docs/WORKFLOW.md` — this project's commands, gates and scripts.
3. `AGENT-WORKFLOW.md` — the general orchestrator/helper method. **Read all of it; don't skip.**
4. `docs/DESIGN.md` — the product and technical design (and `docs/design/mockup.html`).

Helpers (subagents) read `docs/agents/BRIEF.md` before their brief.

Keep HANDOFF, WORKFLOW and DESIGN true: update them in the same commit as any change that makes
them stale. Improve `AGENT-WORKFLOW.md` per its section 12 at the end of every round.
